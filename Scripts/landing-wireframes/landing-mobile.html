Your photos stay yours

BetaRatings and keep or reject marks can also be written to XMP sidecars beside your photos. Culla never changes an image file.

7 The catch

So what’s the catch?

There isn’t one.

I’m Kerim. Culla started as a personal project: a tool I built for my own photos. It turned out to be worth sharing, so I’m sharing it. That’s the whole story.

No paid version is waiting behind this one. No ads, no account, and nothing collected about you or your photos. If Culla is useful to you, good. If something is broken or missing, tell me.
